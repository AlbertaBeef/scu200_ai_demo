SCU200 AI cores: mbdpu01, mbdpu02, mbdpu03 and mbpost

mbdpu01 and mbdpu02 are scalar loops that reload their weights every call and wait for mbpre to stage a 28×28 image in DDR. mbdpu03 reads the camera frame itself, keeps its weights on chip, and feeds a 128-MAC engine from URAM. It does about 34× the work in roughly a quarter of the time. In demo03, mbpost then draws the overlay straight into the HDMI back buffer, which the MicroBlaze used to do.

mbdpu03 (16-lane build)

One core, one pass over the camera crop. A dataflow front end bursts crop rows, extracts pixels and runs conv1 at the same time. Weights load only when ctrl.RELOAD is set; feature maps ping-pong between two URAM banks while one shared engine runs conv2, conv3 and conv4.

Where the cycles go

HLS csynth cycle counts at 100 MHz, every bar on one scale. mbdpu01 and mbdpu02 spend about 80% of their time in conv2: 225,792 multiply-accumulates at one per clock. mbdpu03 does sixteen times the conv work in under a third of the cycles.

data movementconvolutionpool, FC, other

With the input overlapped, conv2 (28k cycles) is now mbdpu03's largest stage. Running the core at 200 MHz would roughly halve every bar. On the board, fcapz measures mbdpu03_infer() at 1.00 ms; that includes the driver's register setup and polling, and the core's camera-read burst alone is 0.162 ms.

How mbdpu03's input path was overlapped

The first mbdpu03 build (45d341f, cff14a2) ran the camera read and conv1 one after the other, and burst in each crop row before extracting any pixel. Commit e0ae0a0 applied both fixes below. Timelines are to scale, blk 1 (112 crop).

mbpost (demo03 overlay renderer)

After each inference, mbpost reads mbdpu03's 112×112 input and logits and redraws the preview, the 10 logit bars and the DX/DY gauges into the HDMI back buffer. It works one aligned row span at a time: burst-read the clean background, compose eight pixels per clock, burst-write. Padding pixels keep the background, so labels in the art are never touched.
