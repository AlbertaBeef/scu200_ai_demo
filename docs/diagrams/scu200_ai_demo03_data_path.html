SCU200 AI demo03: data path

Angle-triggered OV9281 capture with the wake frame dropped in the fabric → DDR → one core that crops and classifies the 112×112 ROI (digit + dx/dy) → a second core that draws the overlay straight into the HDMI back buffer, with dx closing the loop on the strobe angle. Traced from the scu200_ai_demo03 branch (bd_scu200_ai_demo03.tcl, main.c).

Custom core (HLS or Verilog)Shared DDR4Control / trigger / register path

What changed from demo02

mbpre is gone: mbdpu03 reads its crop (112<<blk_shift, box-averaged to 112×112) straight from the camera ring. mbpost replaces the MicroBlaze overlay draw: 1.14 ms instead of 42 ms. fsin_frame_tag drops the OV9281 wake frame before it reaches DDR.

AI traffic through DDR

mbdpu03 reads the crop plus weights/params (0xB0000000 / 0xB0010000), writes the 112×112 input back to 0xB0020000 and out[13] — 10 logits, argmax, dx, dy — to 0xB0030000. mbpost reads both, plus the pristine background at 0xB4000000.

Overlay and display

mbpost composes the 112×112 preview, the 10 logit bars and the DX/DY gauges over the background and burst-writes them to the back buffer (0xB5… / 0xB6…). The MicroBlaze adds the HUD values and page-flips bg_frmbuf_rd. Labels are part of the background image.

Camera timing

VTS 544 (the q key) puts the good frame 10.96 ms after FSIN; it lands in DDR 19.8 ms after FSIN. The drop window in fsin_frame_tag (6010 µs, Q key) separates it from the wake frame at 1.06 ms. This 19.8 ms is now the speed limit: about 380 RPM at 8 captures per revolution.

Clock domains

Camera, DDR and display at 300 MHz; disc_strobe, mbdpu03 and mbpost at 100 MHz, bridged by the SmartConnect.

Auto-target loop

dx is averaged over one revolution and nudges disc_strobe's target angle, one count per 2 px, within a deadband and step cap.

fcapz variant

Read-only monitors feed an fpgacapZero ELA on BSCANE2 USER3. Timelines and the stage-by-stage profile are in docs/profiling/.

Left out

UART console, I²C/clock-chip setup, motor PWM/GPIO, and the ROI capture gallery (dumped over JTAG for retraining).
