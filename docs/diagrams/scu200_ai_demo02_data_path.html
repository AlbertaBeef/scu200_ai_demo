SCU200 AI demo02: data path

Angle-triggered OV9281 capture → DDR → hardware crop/downscale → INT8 CNN (digit + dx/dy) → MicroBlaze overlay → HDMI, with dx closing the loop on the strobe angle. Traced from the scu200_ai_demo02 branch (bd_scu200_ai_demo02.tcl, main.c).

Custom HLS coreShared DDR4Control / trigger / register path

All AI traffic goes through DDR

mbpre writes the 28×28 image to 0xB0020000; mbdpu02 reads it plus weights/params (0xB0000000 / 0xB0010000) and writes out[13] — 10 logits, argmax, dx, dy — to 0xB0030000.

Clock domains

Camera, DDR and display at 300 MHz; disc_strobe, mbpre, mbdpu02 at 100 MHz, bridged by the SmartConnect.

Camera reader

3-slot ring at 0xA0000000 into mixer layer 1 at (160, 500). In trigger mode the firmware holds the classified frame on screen and drops the dim wake frames.

Background reader

1920×1080 image into the mixer master layer. The HUD is drawn into a back buffer (0xB5… / 0xB6…) and page-flipped at the frame boundary.

cpr and target

cpr is encoder counts per disc revolution in Q16. The “20.4:1” gearbox is a four-stage spur train, (22×22×22×23)/(12×10×10×10) = 244904/12000 ≈ 20.409, so 48 CPR × 20.409 ≈ 979.6 counts/rev — the firmware's calibrated value (979.6 × 65536), not the nominal 48 × 20.4 = 979.2. Trimmed live with C/V in 0.1-count steps over 959.6–999.6. target is the angle of the first trigger each revolution; triggers follow at target + k·fire_step, and a new target takes effect at the next wrap.

Auto-target loop

dx is averaged over one revolution and nudges disc_strobe's target angle, about one count per 0.5 px, within a deadband and step cap.

Left out

UART console, I²C/clock-chip setup, motor PWM/GPIO, and the ROI capture gallery at 0xBA000000 (dumped over JTAG for retraining).
